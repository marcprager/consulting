About

Not technical. That's the point.

I lead People Development at Chime, where I'm a team of one responsible for manager development, feedback culture, and IC growth. A couple of years ago I started building AI tools — not because I had an engineering background, but because I had real problems to solve.

What came out of that: production tools, not prototypes. Interactive learning experiences, automated systems, conversation simulators, dashboards — all built without writing a line of code, all deployed to real employees, all still running.

I work with HR and L&D leaders who are sitting on good problems but don't have a clear path from "we should use AI for this" to "here's the working tool." That's the gap I close.
